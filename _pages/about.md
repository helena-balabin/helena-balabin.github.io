I am an incoming postdoctoral associate at the Medical Image Processing and Machine Learning Laboratory (MIPLab) at the University of Calgary, Canada, under supervision of <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ld1C8zUAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>Prof. Nils Forkert</a>, starting in Septemeber 2026. During my postdoc, I will develop vision language model-based digital twins of Alzheimer's disease to simulate disease trajectories, test mechanistic hypotheses and to evaluate targeted interventions.

Previously, I was an interdisciplinary PhD student and Fonds Wetenschappelijk Onderzoek (FWO) fellow at the departments of Neurosciences and Computer Science at KU Leuven in Belgium, jointly supervised by <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lpW2FyoAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>Prof. Rik Vandenberghe</a>, <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=uQkzsEEAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>Prof. Patrick Dupont</a> and <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=O9hYMUUAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>Prof. Marie-Francine (Sien) Moens</a>, and a visiting student at the University of Alberta & the Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute (Amii) in Edmonton, Canada, under supervision of <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Vw8z7qwAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>Prof. Alona Fyshe</a>.

Besides research, I am very passionate about sports in any form, whether it's (half-/ultra-)marathons and trail races, snowboarding, taekwondo or hiking. I also love drum and bass festivals and road trips to remote places.
